First public release of Ottershelf.
- Read EPUB, KEPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, PDF and comics (CBZ, CBR, CB7) from your BookOrbit server, and download books to read offline
- Reading progress and sessions synced with your server
- Highlights, notes, bookmarks, quotes and Look up
- Dashboard, library, series, ISBN scanning and book requests
- Reading timer, calendar, history, statistics, goals and achievements
- Fully open source: no analytics, ads or trackers
