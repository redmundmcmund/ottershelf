Ottershelf is an open-source Android app for reading, tracking and annotating the books on a self-hosted BookOrbit server.

Ottershelf requires your own BookOrbit server, or an account on one, reachable over HTTPS. It contains no books, no store and no server of its own: it shows what your server holds.

Ottershelf is an unofficial, independent app. It is a modified version of BookOrbit and is not an official BookOrbit release, and it is not affiliated with, approved, sponsored or endorsed by the BookOrbit project.

READING
- Ebooks: EPUB, KEPUB, MOBI, AZW3, AZW and FB2, in pages or continuous scrolling, with text size, line spacing, margins, fonts, justification, hyphenation and page colours
- PDF: continuous or single page, fit to width or page, night mode, outline, search and password-protected files
- Comics: CBZ, CBR and CB7, paged or vertical scrolling for webtoons, right-to-left, two-page spreads and zoom
- Highlights in ten colours and four styles, notes, bookmarks and full-text search
- Look up a word or phrase in Wiktionary and Wikipedia, or send it to a dictionary or translation app
- Download books to read offline; downloads continue in the background
- Reading position and sessions sync with your server, and wait on the phone while you are offline

LIBRARY
- Dashboard with BookOrbit's widgets and shelves, arranged the way you like
- All books, libraries, smart scopes, collections, authors and series
- Search, sorting and filters, in a grid or a list
- Book pages with your status, rating, private review and reading log
- A suggestion for the next unread book in a series
- Scan a paper book's ISBN barcode to find it in your library
- Book requests, and editing of titles, authors, series and covers, for accounts with those permissions

TRACKING
- Reading timer, counting up or down, with a notification to pause or stop it
- Log sessions by hand, and record past reads and rereads
- Calendar, reading history, statistics and reading goals
- Achievements and a year in review
- All your highlights and notes in one place: filter, random note, memorize, export as Markdown, share as an image
- Quotes typed in, or read from a photo of the page on the phone

APPEARANCE
- Light, dark or system theme, accent colours, corner radius and background patterns, optionally synced with your account

PRIVACY
- No analytics, ads, crash reporting or trackers
- The app connects only to the server you enter, and to Wiktionary and Wikipedia when you tap Look up, without your account's token
- The camera is used only for ISBN scanning, page photos and cover photos, and images are processed on the phone
- HTTPS only, and sign-in tokens are encrypted on the phone
